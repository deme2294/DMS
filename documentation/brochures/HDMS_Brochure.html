System Overview

What is HDMS?

The ITPC Help Desk Service Application built to simplify how support requests are submitted, routed, solved, and verified.

HDMS (Help Desk Management System) is the official digital platform powering the ITPC Help Desk Service Application. It replaces scattered emails, phone calls, and verbal follow-ups with one organized service desk where every issue is recorded as a trackable ticket.

The system brings five user groups into one connected workflow: Tenants (service requesters), Staff (technical/support responders), Department Managers (team leads), System Administrators (routing & configuration operators), and the CEO (executive oversight). Everyone sees a clean workspace tailored strictly to their duties.

Built with React, Node.js, MariaDB, and Socket.IO, HDMS combines structured ticket lifecycles, automatic SLA deadline tracking, real-time chat, WebRTC video conferencing, a versioned Knowledge Base, and department performance analytics in one application.

Role-based workspaces for CEO, Admin, Manager, Staff & Tenant
SLA timers with early, on-time & late tracking plus auto-escalation
Built-in live chat, threaded replies & WebRTC video calls
Public tracking number, Knowledge Base & service ratings
Department-scoped analytics, reports & full audit log
End-to-End Application Flow

How HDMS Works — From Start to Finish

Follow the complete journey of a service request in the ITPC Help Desk Service Application — from setup and submission to resolution, tenant confirmation, and reporting.

1. Tenant Submits Ticket
2. Admin Routes to Dept
3. Manager Assigns Staff
4. Staff Solves & Updates
5. Manager Marks Complete
6. Tenant Confirms & Rates
7. Closed & Archived
Stage 1 • Setup

System Readiness

Actor: System Admin

Before tickets arrive, the Administrator configures Departments, registers Users & Tenants, defines Ticket Categories, and sets SLA Policies (target first-response, escalation, and resolution hours by priority and category).

Departments & Users Ready SLA Rules Active
Stage 2 • Intake

Self-Check or Create Ticket

Actor: Tenant

A Tenant first checks the Knowledge Base or Notices for quick answers. If support is needed, they open New Ticket, enter a title and description, pick a Category and Priority (previewing the expected SLA deadline), optionally select a Department, and attach up to 5 files. HDMS issues a Tracking Number usable even without logging in.

Status: Submitted / Open Public Tracking Link Generated
Stage 3 • Triage

Review & Route to Department

Actor: Admin / CEO

The Admin reviews the new ticket, adjusts Priority or Category if needed, merges any duplicate tickets, and routes it to the responsible Department and Department Manager. (Tickets submitted directly to a department enter that department's queue immediately.)

Status: Assigned Department Department Manager Notified
Stage 4 • Dispatch

Department Intake & Staff Assignment

Actor: Department Manager

The Department Manager reviews the routed ticket and either Accepts it into the department queue or Rejects it with a reason. Once accepted, the Manager assigns (or reassigns) it to an available Staff member and monitors capacity via the Matrix View and department dashboards.

Status: Assigned Staff Staff Reminder & Reassign Tools
Stage 5 • Execution

Investigation, Chat & Fix

Actor: Assigned Staff

Staff accepts the assignment, switches status to In Progress, and works on the problem. During troubleshooting, Staff can set sub-statuses (Waiting Tenant or Waiting Vendor), upload up to 10 attachments, write private Internal Notes, post visibility-controlled Threaded Replies, message in Real-Time Chat, or start an in-app WebRTC Video Call. When fixed, Staff marks it Under Review / Pending Approval.

In Progress → Under Review Chat, Video & File Sharing
Stage 6 • SLA Guard

SLA Monitoring & Escalation

Actor: System / Admin / Manager

While the ticket is open, HDMS tracks first-response and resolution deadlines against the assigned SLA policy. If a deadline is approached or breached, or if the issue is critical, Admin or Manager triggers Escalation — elevating visibility to leadership (Admin & CEO) until resolved.

Status: Escalated (if breached) Early / On-Time / Late Tracked
Stage 7 • Verification

Department Completion & Approval

Actor: Manager & Admin

The Department Manager verifies the staff member's solution and marks the ticket Completed. Admin or Manager approves completion, advancing the ticket to Resolved / Approved and notifying the Tenant to verify the outcome.

Status: Completed → Resolved Tenant Notified to Confirm
Stage 8 • Sign-Off

Tenant Confirmation & Closure

Actor: Tenant

The Tenant reviews the fix. If satisfied, they Confirm Resolution and submit a 1–5 Star Rating & Feedback, transitioning the ticket to Closed (inactive and locked from further assignment). If the problem was not solved, the Tenant can Reject Completion so it returns to active work.

Status: Closed (Final) Service Quality Rated (1–5 Stars)
Stage 9 • Follow-Through

Reopening, Knowledge & Analytics

Actor: All Roles / CEO

If a closed issue recurs, the Tenant submits a Reopen Request (reason required), which Admin, Manager, or CEO can Approve (moving the ticket to Reopened) or Reject. Meanwhile, reusable fixes are published in the Knowledge Base, and every action feeds real-time Department & Executive Analytics and the Audit Log.

Reopen Control KB Articles Published Strict SLA Performance Scored

Escalation Branch

  1. Triggered manually by Admin/Manager or automatically on SLA breach
  2. Ticket status changes to Escalated with priority visibility
  3. Admin and CEO receive immediate alerts for executive oversight
  4. Returns to active processing once reassigned or resolved

Reopen & Hold Branch

  1. Closed tickets cannot be assigned unless formally reopened
  2. Tenant submits a Reopen Request with a mandatory reason
  3. Admin, Manager, or CEO approves → status becomes Reopened
  4. Active holds use Waiting Tenant or Waiting Vendor sub-statuses

Roles & Responsibilities

What Each Role Does in HDMS

Every user in the ITPC Help Desk Service Application has clear duties enforced by role-based access control.

CEO / Executive

Strategic Oversight
  • View Executive Dashboard & organization-wide KPIs
  • Compare all departments in Executive Analytics
  • Review Executive Approvals & escalations
  • Monitor Feedback Oversight & satisfaction
  • Approve/reject ticket reopen requests & overrides
  • Export executive reports & inspect Audit Logs

System Admin

Configuration & Routing
  • Manage Users, Tenants, Departments & Matrix
  • Configure SLA Policies & Ticket Categories
  • Review new tickets & route to departments
  • Merge duplicate tickets & override assignments
  • Publish, archive & manage KB Categories/Stats
  • Publish Notices, export Reports & Audit Logs

Department Manager

Department Head
  • Accept or reject tickets routed to their department
  • Assign, reassign & remind department Staff
  • Monitor department-only SLA & Early/On-Time/Late score
  • Mark tickets completed & approve reopen requests
  • Escalate overdue or blocked tickets upward
  • Approve KB articles & manage Feedback Desk

Staff Member

Support Responder
  • Accept or decline assigned department tickets
  • Investigate & update status (In Progress / Under Review)
  • Use Waiting Tenant / Waiting Vendor when blocked
  • Post threaded replies, internal notes & attachments
  • Chat & join WebRTC video meetings with users
  • Draft Knowledge Base articles & handle feedback tasks

Tenant

Service Requester
  • Create support tickets with attachments (up to 5)
  • Track ticket status live (login or tracking number)
  • Receive real-time alerts & chat with support staff
  • Confirm resolution & submit 1–5 star rating
  • Reject incomplete work or request ticket reopening
  • Read Notices & browse self-service Knowledge Base
