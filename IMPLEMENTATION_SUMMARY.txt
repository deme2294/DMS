🎯 HOW IT WORKS:

1. TENANT CLICKS EMAIL LINK
   ↓
2. generateEmailTemplate() FUNCTION CREATES TEMPLATE
   - Builds subject line
   - Formats body with office details
   - Adds question categories
   - Encodes for mailto: URL
   ↓
3. EMAIL CLIENT OPENS WITH PRE-FILLED TEMPLATE
   ↓
4. TENANT CUSTOMIZES:
   - Checks relevant questions ☑
   - Adds specific needs
   - Fills in contact info
   ↓
5. TENANT SENDS EMAIL
   ↓
6. OFFICE CONTACT RECEIVES STRUCTURED INQUIRY
   ↓
7. OFFICE CONTACT RESPONDS WITH ANSWERS
